NEURAL BENCHMARKS // 5-MODEL HETEROGENEOUS ENSEMBLE

Model Architectures Deep-Dive

Detailed mechanical rationale for individual backbone selection, inductive biases, receptive fields, and marine morphological extraction specializations.

DINOv2 (ViT-L/14)
304M Params

Vision foundation model trained with self-supervised teacher-student ViT. Excels at invariant dense visual representations, providing robust morphometric embeddings irrespective of lighting anomalies or turbidity.

Primary Target:Morphometric invariant patterns
Self-Attention:Global All-to-All
Standalone Top-1:96.8%
Ensemble Weight Contribution: 0.32
ViT-B/16
86M Params

86M parameter global self-attention mechanism capturing long-range spatial relationships. Ideal for evaluating macro body contours, proportional aspect ratios, and spatial relationships between dorsal and anal fins.

Primary Target:Macro anatomy & silhouette
Patch Dimensions:16x16 tokens
Standalone Top-1:94.7%
Ensemble Weight Contribution: 0.24
ConvNeXt Tiny
28M Params

Modern convolutional inductive bias with depthwise separable 7x7 convolutions. Excels at high-frequency localized scale textures, dermal denticles, fin ray segmentations, and fine chromatographic pixel clusters.

Primary Target:Scale microtextures & fin rays
Inductive Bias:Spatial translation invariance
Standalone Top-1:95.1%
Ensemble Weight Contribution: 0.22
Swin Transformer
50M Params

Shifted window hierarchical attention with linear computational complexity. Dynamically crosses scales to correlate global swimming posture with localized operculum and lateral line curvature.

Primary Target:Multi-scale body segment ratios
Attention Type:Shifted Windows (W-MSA)
Standalone Top-1:94.9%
Ensemble Weight Contribution: 0.14
MobileNetV3-Large
5.4M Params

Optimized inverted bottlenecks with Hard-Swish activations and squeeze-and-excitation blocks. Serves as our primary candidate for onboard battery-constrained autonomous ROV and benthic lander deployments.

Primary Target:Ultra-low latency edge inference
Hardware Target:Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano
Standalone Top-1:90.3%
Ensemble Weight Contribution: 0.08
