auto_awesome Scientific Curiosities

change_circle Ontogenetic Color Metamorphosis

Post-larval juveniles bear almost zero blue pigment: they emerge as bright fluorescent yellow bodies with pale cyan highlights surrounding the eye sockets, completely transforming into saturated royal blue as they reach 45 days post-settlement.

bedtime Akinetic Nocturnal Decumbency

Blue tangs display tonic immobility sleep patterns; they frequently rest completely flat on their sides within micro-cavities of coral rubble, often appearing lifeless to uninformed divers.
